# B1 — The Glacier cost gotcha

What's the actual cost impact of an early deletion after archival? How would you redesign the
lifecycle so this stops quietly costing more than budgeted?

<!-- Your answer here -->

Problem:
Our lifecycle rule moves photos to Glacier after 30 days to save money. But Glacier has a catch: it charges for at least 90 days, even if you delete the file sooner. Sellers can ask us to delete their photos, and we must do it within 30 days. So if a photo goes to Glacier on day 30 and the seller asks for deletion on day 40, we still pay for 80 more days. On top of that, the rule has no filter, so it archives every photo, not just sold listings. Nothing fails or raises an error. The cost just quietly grows on the bill.

How I found it:
I used AWS Cost Explorer to find this cost. I filtered by the S3 service and grouped by usage type. That showed Glacier early-delete charges that were not in our budget.

solution:
I would make four changes. First, I'd add a filter so only sold listings' photos are moved. Second, I'd use Intelligent-Tiering instead of Glacier, because it has no minimum storage time and no early-delete fee. Third, I'd skip very small files like thumbnails, because the per-file fees cost more than we save. Fourth, I'd add a budget alert so we catch these charges early instead of finding them at the end of the month.
